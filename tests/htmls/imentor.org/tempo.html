What iMentor offers

Professional Development

We offer a seamless and comprehensive onboarding experience and a generous professional development budget that staff can use toward courses, conferences, and publications.

Culture

Recognized as one of "The Best Nonprofits to Work For" by Opportunity Knocks and The Nonprofit Times, we are committed to sustaining a thriving, celebratory organizational culture and providing competitive compensation and benefits to our staff.

Individuality

We welcome and appreciate the differences between individuals and we celebrate that as one of our core strengths.
